The Lectorium — an open university of life

Courses of lectures on what everyday life is made of: fear, decisions, relationships, money, thinking. Open in full — free, no sign-up, no paywall.

Each course is a short series of lectures with diagrams and practice, built from the simple to the complex. The lectures are in Russian; browser translation works well enough to follow them.

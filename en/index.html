position

Education lags behind the science of thinking

Over half a century, cognitive science has learned how people understand, remember and decide. Understanding rests on a framework of concepts, not on a sum of facts. Knowledge sticks when it is used, not when it is reread. Thinking errs predictably — and that can be taught.

The education system barely takes this into account. It teaches the way it did a hundred years ago.

The Lectorium, Variatika and the Conceptual Base are my answer: teaching assembled from these findings.

Andrey Meyster

Fredi — a virtual psychologist

Talk at any hour — in English or in Russian. Fredi listens, asks questions and helps you see which story you are living in, and where it isn’t yours. Built by Andrey Meyster, who also wrote its code.

The first 15 minutes are free, no sign-up. After that — a subscription (prices in roubles). The app interface is in Russian for now: just write to Fredi in English.
